\section{What is the Number $e$?}
Here are three equivalent ways to understand the number $e$.

\begin{enumerate}
\item It is the number that makes the tangent line to the curve $y=e^x$ at the point $(0,1)$ have slope $1$.

\item It is the number that makes the population function
\[
   P = A e^t \, , \, t \in \mathbb{R},
\] 
increase at a constant relative instantaneous rate of $100\%$/hr. Here we are assuming $t$ is measured in hours and that $A>0$ is the population at time $t=0$.

\item The number $e$ as a limit:
\[
  e = \lim_{n\to \infty} \left( 1 + \frac{1}{n} \right)^n.
\]
\end{enumerate}

\begin{question} \label{Qdefer3rv}
This question is about description (b).

The function
\[
    P = f(t) = 1000e^{0.25t} \, , \,  0\leq t \leq 8 ,
\]
expresses the balance in an account (measured in dollars) in terms of the number of years since the start of 2026.

\begin{enumerate}

\item Find the one-year growth factor.

\item Find the four-year growth factor.

\item Use your result from part (b) to find the relative instantaneous growth rate.

The relative instantaneous growth rate is
\[
    \frac{100\%}{\answer{4}\text{ years}} = \answer{25}\% / \text{year}.
\]

\item Verify your answer to part (c) with the chain rule.
\end{enumerate}
\end{question}

\begin{question} \label{Qpvem3r34}
The function
\[
        P = 250 (1.1)^t \, , \, 0\leq t \leq 10,
\]
expresses the population of a colony of bacteria (in millions) in terms of the number of hours past noon.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Show algebraically that the relative average rate of change in the population from time $t=a$ to time $t=b>a$ hours past noon depends only on the difference $b-a$.

\item Use your expression from part (a) to write the relative instantaneous growth rate of the population as a limit.

\item Interpret your expresssion from part (b) geometrically as the slope of some tangent line.

\item Use numerical methods to approximate the relative instantaneous growth rate.

\item Find the exact relative instantaneous growth rate.

\end{enumerate}
\end{question}


\begin{question} \label{Qeirefr}
\item The balance in an account increases exponentially. The account has $\$5,000$ at the start of 2026 and $\$5600$ three months later.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Find the 3-month growth factor. Do \emph{not} use $e$.

\item Find the exact one-year growth factor. Do \emph{not} use $e$.

\item Find an expression for the function $B=f(t)$ that expresses the balance in the account (in dollars) in terms of the number of years since the start of 2026. Do \emph{not} use $e$.

\item Rewrite your expression for the function $B=f(t)$ using the number $e$.

\item Find a function $r=g(B)$ that expresses the growth rate (measured in dollars/yr) in terms of the balance (measured in dollars).

\item At what (absolute) rate is the balance changing when the account has $\$10,000$?

\item Find the balance when it is increasing at the rate of $\$2000$/yr.

\end{enumerate}

\end{question}
